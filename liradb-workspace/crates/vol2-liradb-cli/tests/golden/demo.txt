LiraDB — demo del motor (caps. 17-21: parse → lower → optimizador → Volcano)
Grafo demo: 6 nodos (Person/City) y 6 aristas (KNOWS/LIVES_IN)

── [1/4] MATCH simple (NodeScan + Project) ──
LiraQL: MATCH (p:Person) RETURN p.name, p.age
Plan lógico:
Project(p.name, p.age)
  NodeScan(Person AS p)
Resultado:
p.name  | p.age
"Ana"   | 36   
"Bo"    | 41   
"Carla" | 29   
"Dani"  | 36   
Métricas: Project: 4 filas
NodeScan: 4 filas
filas devueltas: 4

── [2/4] WHERE con comparación (Filter trivalente) ──
LiraQL: MATCH (p:Person) WHERE p.age < 40 RETURN p.name, p.age
Plan lógico:
Project(p.name, p.age)
  Filter(p.age < 40)
    NodeScan(Person AS p)
Resultado:
p.name  | p.age
"Ana"   | 36   
"Carla" | 29   
"Dani"  | 36   
Métricas: Project: 3 filas
Filter: 3 filas
NodeScan: 4 filas
filas devueltas: 3

── [3/4] Patrón relacional (Expand) ──
LiraQL: MATCH (p:Person)-[:KNOWS]->(f:Person) RETURN p.name, f.name
Plan lógico:
Project(p.name, f.name)
  Filter(f:Person)
    Expand(p, KNOWS, OUTGOING, f)
      NodeScan(Person AS p)
Resultado:
p.name  | f.name 
"Ana"   | "Bo"   
"Bo"    | "Carla"
"Carla" | "Ana"  
"Dani"  | "Dani" 
Métricas: Project: 4 filas
Filter: 4 filas
Expand: 4 filas
NodeScan: 4 filas
filas devueltas: 4

── [4/4] Consulta canónica del brief + propiedad de arista ──
LiraQL: MATCH (p:Person)-[r:KNOWS]->(f:Person) WHERE p.name = "Ana" RETURN f.name, r.since
Plan lógico:
Project(f.name, r.since)
  Filter(f:Person AND p.name = "Ana")
    Expand(p, r:KNOWS, OUTGOING, f)
      NodeScan(Person AS p)
Resultado:
f.name | r.since
"Bo"   | 2020   
Métricas: Project: 1 filas
Filter: 1 filas
Expand: 4 filas
NodeScan: 4 filas
filas devueltas: 1
